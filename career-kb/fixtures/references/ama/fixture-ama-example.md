## Overview

This is a synthetic, entirely invented practitioner AMA used only as a test
fixture for `@magistr/career-kb`. It does not describe any real person's career,
employer, or transition.

## Key Constructs & Definitions

**Intentional pivot** -- a deliberately planned move into an adjacent field, as
opposed to a reactive one forced by circumstance.

**Transferable skills** -- the subset of a fixture-candidate's skill set that
maps cleanly onto the target field's job descriptions.

## Main Findings & Arguments

A fixture practitioner argues that informational interviews conducted before a
pivot reduce the perceived risk of the move more than additional credentialing
does.

## Practical Implications

Run five fixture informational interviews before updating a resume.
